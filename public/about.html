About Us

The story behind the world's #1 foiling podcast.

Our Mission

The Generic Foiling Podcast was born out of a shared obsession with hydrofoiling in all its forms. Whether it's wing foiling, prone, downwind, kite, SUP, foil-assist or parafoiling, we're here to talk about it.

Each week we break down the latest gear, share session stories, interview top riders and industry figures, and try to answer the questions you actually care about.

No scripts. No sponsors telling us what to say. Just two mates who love foiling, a couple of microphones, and the occasional beer.
