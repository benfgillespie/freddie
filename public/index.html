Meet the Hosts

Freddie von Oldfield

The Sales Rep

Sells your favourite watersports gear by day, talks about riding it the rest of the time. Avid water sports enthusiast turned farmer turned water sports enthusiast again most recently. Currently Parawing obsessed but enjoys a surf, a spearfish or a bike ride when conditions allow.

Liam Proctor

The Engineer

Brings the technical brain to the show — gear, hydrodynamics, and why your foil setup is probably wrong. Keeps Freddie honest. Liam spent a bunch of years teaching water sports around the world before settling back into Engineering back in his home town.
