Show Hosts

Your loyal Generic Foiling Podcast Co-Hosts, Prince Harry & Fred Dibnah

Liam Proctor

The Engineer

Watersports instructor, turned engineer, turned watersports instructor, turned engineer. At 33, he’s certainly had more jobs than most! Liam brings the technical, analytical element to the podcast and brings Freddie back on track when he loses focus. A severe board building interest and engineering mindset gives Liam the technical knowhow to put the squeeze on the big dogs we end up chatting to.

  • Likes: Bananas, coffee & old crap cars
  • Dislikes: Rules, being told what to do & queuing

Frederick von Oldfield

The Sales Rep

A completely average riding skillset meant Freddie had to settle for a job in the industry in order to stay involved. His main role is to make sure Liam doesn’t go too deep and techy too often as his little brain can’t handle it. Freddie’s interests are in the pro riders and the business side of things, and he aims to give listeners a little inside knowledge of how their favourite brands operate.

  • Likes: Cholula, cooking, rugby & beer
  • Dislikes: Downwind paddling, road rage & Duotone
